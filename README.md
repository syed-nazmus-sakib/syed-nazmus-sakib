I am a final-year Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Dhaka. I am a Research Assistant at [Cortex AI Lab](https://cortexairg.com/) and a Research Intern at the [Data and Design Lab (CARS)](https://www.dndlab.org/). I also collaborate with [Prof. Zehong (Jimmy) Cao](https://czh513.github.io/) at Adelaide University on reinforcement learning and planning with learned world models.

My research asks how intelligent systems **reason, make decisions, and fail**, with a focus on multimodal and agentic AI, agent safety, and reinforcement learning.

[Website](https://syed-nazmus-sakib.github.io/) · [CV](https://syed-nazmus-sakib.github.io/cv/Syed_Nazmus_Sakib.pdf) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=4j76UvsAAAAJ&hl=en) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/nazmus-sakib-syed) · [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/syednazmussakib)

## Currently working on

- **Long-horizon planning with latent world models:** graph-based subgoal selection, goal representations, and planning under learned dynamics.
- **Counterfactual surgical video generation:** physically and temporally consistent scene changes.
- **LLM reasoning:** reinforcement learning for reasoning and reliable evaluation.
- **Goal-conditioned and multi-agent RL:** representation bottlenecks and information scope in cooperative learning.
